## Recording AI help

1. **Source files.** Every file you write or change starts with one of the three levels:
   `No substantial AI-generated code`, `Below 50% AI-generated` or `50% or more AI-generated`.
   Code first generated by AI stays AI-generated after you edit it.
2. **Pull request.** The `## AI contribution` section gives one level and lists the AI-assisted files.
3. **Wiki.** For a feature or fix built with AI help, add an entry to the wiki page of the iteration
   whose milestone holds the issue, for example
   [Iteration 4 – AI Usage](https://github.com/B-A-D-R-SL/iris/wiki/Iteration-4-%E2%80%93-AI-Usage).
   Add it when you open the pull request, then add the review corrections before it merges.
   Add a row for it to the table at the top of that page too.

## Entry format

```markdown
### <ID> <title>

**Story** [#<issue>](https://github.com/B-A-D-R-SL/iris/issues/<issue>) · **Pull request** [#<pr>](https://github.com/B-A-D-R-SL/iris/pull/<pr>) · **Directed by** @<login> · **AI tool** <tool> · **Level** <level>

**What AI helped with.** One paragraph, at most 80 words: what the AI produced and what you decided.

**How we verified it**

- The tests that check the AI-generated behaviour (file or case names) and what they check.
- Review comments that questioned the code, with links.

**Problems found and corrections**

- What was wrong in the AI output and how it was fixed, or a suggestion you rejected and why.

<details>
<summary>Files with substantial AI help</summary>

- `path/to/file.py`

</details>
```

give the finished entry to the developer to paste into that wiki page.
